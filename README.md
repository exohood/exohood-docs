## Welcome to Exohood Labs

Exohood Labs is a privately operated research and development company founded in 2020.

Our work focuses on the investigation, design, and development of software, computational systems, and emerging technologies with practical applications across industries and scientific disciplines.

We believe that complex technological knowledge should be explained clearly, supported by technical reasoning, and made accessible to those who wish to understand it.

The Exohood documentation portal provides a central resource for researchers, developers, students, technical professionals, and readers interested in technology.

Our research covers subjects ranging from artificial intelligence and decentralized systems to computational science, graphics, energy, health, robotics, and space exploration.

**Our approach combines scientific curiosity with practical engineering and responsible technology development.**

## Decentralized Finance & Exoswap

Exohood Labs researches decentralized finance and develops Exoswap, a non custodial decentralized exchange application.

Exoswap integrates external liquidity aggregation and routing infrastructure, including 0x, to provide access to supported digital asset swaps.

Its architecture focuses on:

- Web3 wallet connectivity.
- User authorized transactions.
- External liquidity routing.
- Supported EVM blockchain networks.
- Token discovery and contract identification.
- Blockchain transaction verification.
- Market reference information.
- Integration with independent cross chain providers.

Exoswap does not need to operate proprietary liquidity pools to access decentralized markets.

## Research Methodology

Our research approach emphasizes the importance of combining technical investigation with practical application.

The methodology addresses several interconnected principles:

**Scientific Inquiry**

Developing questions, evaluating hypotheses, and examining technical evidence.

**Interdisciplinary Research**

Exploring connections between computer science, mathematics, engineering, and other scientific fields.

**Responsible Development**

Considering the potential security, ethical, environmental, and social implications of technological research.

**Practical Application**

Investigating how scientific and technical concepts may contribute to useful software and engineering solutions.

**Critical Evaluation**

Distinguishing established results from theoretical proposals, simulations, experimental systems, and unsupported assumptions.

**Knowledge Sharing**

Publishing selected research and educational information to encourage informed understanding and independent learning.

[Read the Research Methodology](https://docs.exohood.com/methodology-of-research)

## Understanding Project Status

The documentation includes publications from different periods of Exohood Labs' development history.

Some describe active projects, while others refer to experiments, early prototypes, discontinued initiatives, or long term research proposals.

To avoid confusion, the following distinctions are important.

| Classification | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Active Project | A project with current development or operational activity |
| Research | Scientific or technical work that may not result in a deployed product |
| Experimental | Concepts, prototypes, or implementations requiring further validation |
| Historical | Earlier documentation preserved for reference |
| Discontinued | A project or service for which official development has ended |
| Proprietary | Technology governed by private intellectual property and access restrictions |
| Public Source Code | Published code governed by its applicable repository license |

A document describing a product, API, token, blockchain, or technical service does not by itself establish that the service is currently available.

Historical roadmap milestones and technical proposals should not be treated as operational commitments.

Readers should consult the relevant project's current official sources before relying on implementation instructions, financial information, or deployment guidance.
